# High-Cash-Intensity Customer Risk Rating Criteria

## Purpose
Defines the weighted factors used to rate a customer or product change as Low, Medium, High, or Critical risk when cash intensity is a material factor, as it is for cannabis-related businesses, check-cashing services, and other cash-heavy customer types.

## Weighted Factors
Risk score is a weighted sum of five factors, each scored 0-4 and multiplied by its weight: cash percentage of total deposits (weight 0.30), jurisdiction risk rating (weight 0.20), screening hit severity (weight 0.25), product or customer-type novelty (weight 0.15), and prior examination findings for comparable business lines (weight 0.10).

## Tier Thresholds
A weighted total of 0.0-1.0 is Low risk, 1.01-2.0 is Medium, 2.01-3.0 is High, and above 3.0 is Critical. Any unresolved sanctions or PEP hit automatically floors the case at High regardless of the weighted total.

## Cash Percentage Scoring
Cash percentage of total deposits is scored: under 20% scores 0, 20-40% scores 1, 40-60% scores 2, 60-80% scores 3, and above 80% scores 4. Cannabis dispensaries typically score 3 or 4 on this factor given limited access to card processing.

## Documentation Requirement
Every risk score must be accompanied by a factor-by-factor breakdown showing the raw score, the weight applied, and the resulting contribution, so the total is auditable and explainable rather than a single opaque number.
