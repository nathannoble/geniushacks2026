# New Product or Service Change Review Procedure

## Purpose
Establishes the required Financial Crimes Risk Management (FCRM) review process before Commercial Banking, Retail Banking, or any other business line launches a new product, enters a new geography, or begins serving a new customer segment.

## Submission Requirements
The business line raising the change must submit: a description of the change, the target geography, the target customer segment, the proposed launch date, and any supporting documents (product specifications, vendor contracts, marketing copy, or process diagrams) relevant to evaluating financial crime risk.

## Review Stages
Every submission proceeds through intake and validation, document and policy enrichment, risk scoring, analyst review, and committee decision, in that order. A case may be returned to the submitting business line as a request for information (RFI) at analyst review if required information is missing or unclear; it does not proceed to committee review until the analyst finalizes it.

## Decision Outcomes
The committee records exactly one of four outcomes for each case: Approved, Rejected, Deferred, or Approved with Conditions. Approved with Conditions requires the specific conditions to be documented and tracked; the business line may not launch until FCRM confirms the conditions have been met.

## Target Timeline
FCRM targets a two-business-day turnaround from submission to committee decision for standard-complexity changes. Cases requiring extended enhanced due diligence, such as cannabis-related business changes, may take longer, but every case must still pass through both the analyst review and committee decision gates regardless of timeline pressure.
