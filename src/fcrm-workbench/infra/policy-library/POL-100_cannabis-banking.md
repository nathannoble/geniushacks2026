# Cannabis-Related Business Banking Policy

## Scope
This policy governs the bank's approach to opening and maintaining deposit accounts for cannabis-related businesses (CRBs), including licensed dispensaries, cultivators, processors, and ancillary vendors, in states where cannabis is legal under state law.

## Federal-State Conflict
Cannabis remains a Schedule I controlled substance under the federal Controlled Substances Act, regardless of state legalization. Because the bank is federally regulated, any CRB relationship must be evaluated under enhanced due diligence, consistent with FinCEN guidance on marijuana-related businesses (synthetic reference: FIN-2014-G001-S).

## Required Conditions for Approval
A CRB account may be approved only if the business holds a current state license, operates exclusively within that state's legal cannabis program, and agrees to: (1) quarterly transaction-volume attestations, (2) enhanced transaction monitoring at a lower dollar threshold than standard commercial accounts, and (3) an annual re-verification of licensure.

## Risk Tiering
CRB accounts are rated no lower than Medium risk regardless of other factors, reflecting the underlying federal-state conflict. Accounts are rated High or Critical when any of the following apply: cash represents more than 70% of deposits, the business operates in more than one state, or screening identifies an unresolved adverse-media or watchlist match for any owner or beneficial owner.

## Prohibited Relationships
The bank will not open accounts for cannabis businesses operating in states without a legal adult-use or medical program, for businesses lacking current state licensure, or for any business with an unresolved sanctions or PEP hit above the bank's screening threshold.
