# Precedent Case: State-Legal Hemp Processor Approved With Conditions

## Summary
In a prior case, Commercial Banking requested approval to bank a state-licensed hemp processing facility in Oregon. Hemp is federally legal under the 2018 Farm Bill when THC content stays below 0.3%, distinguishing this case from cannabis dispensary banking, though FCRM applied similar enhanced due diligence given the adjacent industry and historical cash intensity.

## Risk Assessment Outcome
The case was rated Medium risk. Cash represented 45% of deposits, jurisdiction risk was Low (single-state operation in a state with an established regulatory program), and screening returned no hits on any named owner or beneficial owner.

## Committee Decision
Approved with Conditions. Conditions included quarterly attestations of continued compliance with the 0.3% THC threshold, enhanced monitoring for the first two quarters of the relationship, and immediate escalation if the business expanded into cannabis products exceeding the legal THC threshold.

## Relevance to Future Cases
This precedent is relevant to any case involving hemp, CBD, or adjacent plant-based products where the federal-state legal status differs from cannabis dispensary banking, and illustrates that Approved with Conditions is the typical outcome for cash-intensive, single-state, no-hit cases in this category.
